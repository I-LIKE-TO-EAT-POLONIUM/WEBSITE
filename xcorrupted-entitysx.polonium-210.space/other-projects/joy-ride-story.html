Joy Ride Story

Placeholder / W.I.P. — this is planning notes only, not a finished story. Premise: a haunted taxi (a mint-condition 1980s Crown Victoria belonging to a defunct company called Joy Ride Cab Co.) still picks up fares around Essex Junction, Vermont, driven by a man who died in a 1993 head-on collision and doesn't know it. Passengers who glance in the rearview mirror see him as a skeleton. Planned as a short SFM animation, likely starting with just the "first ride" scene as an MVP before any larger scope.

The Car

  • Vehicle: 1980s Ford Crown Victoria, mint condition despite the era — no rust, no wear, always clean regardless of weather.
  • Colors: deep oxblood/maroon red body with black roof, trim, and wheels.
  • Livery: "Joy Ride Cab Co." in black script on the door, beside a black-outline sun mascot that reads as more gothic than cheerful.
  • Registration: plate lapsed on a normal cycle in 1995 (nobody left to renew it); the company itself limped on until bankruptcy in 2009.

Setting

  • Location: Essex Junction, Vermont — back roads out toward the Winooski River.
  • Season: Spring mud season, snow mid-thaw, river running high, brown slush against a landscape that hasn't greened up yet.
  • Locations needed: back road (cab interior + exterior), the old Joy Ride garage (1993 active / 2015 defunct), crash site, a diner, Pelkey's cruiser.

Story Order (W.I.P.)

  1. Part One — March 1993, daytime (Sal's POV): Sal's ordinary morning shift at the Joy Ride garage; a few routine fares; dispatch radios him the fare he'll never reach. Ends before anything ominous happens.
  2. Part Two — 2015 (Mo's POV): Mo's low point and return to Essex Junction; her car gets stuck on a back road at night during the thaw; the cab arrives; the first ride, including the rearview mirror moment; aftermath and the town's reluctance to talk about "the cab."
  3. Part Three — 2015 (Officer Pelkey's POV): Pelkey spots the Crown Vic on a routine night, runs the plate, traces it to Joy Ride Cab Co. (bankrupt 2009), does the stop, runs Sal's license — and the name stops him cold. Car and driver are gone by the time it registers.
  4. Part Four — March 1993, flashback: Triggered by the name. Picks up the same day as Part One, that evening — Sal heading to the fare, sleet, the river high, a truck crossing the center line. The collision (handled with restraint). Ends on 21-year-old Pelkey arriving at the scene as a rookie.
  5. Part Five — Convergence (2015): Mo and Pelkey compare accounts; digging into the Joy Ride paperwork trail with Ruthie's help; identifying the 1993 fare; finding a way to complete it; a final, calmer encounter with the cab; the haunting ends (or seems to) as mud season gives way to spring.

Production Notes (W.I.P.)

  • Planned medium: Source Filmmaker (SFM).
  • MVP scene to build first: the "first ride" (Part Two) — stranded Mo, the cab's arrival, the ride, the mirror reveal, the drop-off. Self-contained, two characters, minimal locations.
  • Casting from stock Half-Life 2 models: generic/older-looking citizen models reskinned for Sal (red Joy Ride jacket) and Ruthie; a citizen model with a police reskin for Pelkey (works for both his 1993 and 2015 versions); a generic female citizen model (not Alyx) for Mo, to keep her reading as an original character.
  • Skeleton reveal: swap to a skeleton model sharing Sal's rig, shown only via a second camera faking the mirror reflection, rather than a real-time reflection/morph.
  • Maps: custom-built in Hammer, loosely based on real reference photos/Street View of Essex Junction back roads, built as modular/separate pieces (likely separate compiled maps per location) so the whole map never has to load at once.
