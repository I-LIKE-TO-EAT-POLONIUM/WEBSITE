7. Command Structure

  • Command – 4–10 Legions
  • Legion – 3–6 Corps
  • Corps – 2–8 Divisions
  • Division – 12–24 Brigades
  • Brigade – 12–24 Regiments
  • Regiment – 12–24 Battalions
  • Battalion – 24–32 Companies
  • Company – 24–32 Units
  • Unit – 24–32 Platoons
  • Platoon – 32–44 Troops
  • Troop – 8–12 Groups
  • Group – 8–12 Squads
  • Squad – 8–12 Teams
  • Team – 10–16 People
