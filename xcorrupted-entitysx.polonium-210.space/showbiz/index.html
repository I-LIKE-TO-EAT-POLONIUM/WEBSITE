Company Info & History

STT Inc. was established on February 9th, 1969, in Pine Ridge, Evergreen (S.A.D.), Algrovik Island. The company's first location was acquired the following year, on October 6th, 1970.

The company operates 5 locations, including 1 small HQ office, all located within the fictional Nordic country of Ălgrøvík.

See the Locations section for full HQ and per-location details.

1969 — The Founding Year

STT Inc.'s first year was spent almost entirely on groundwork rather than construction — incorporation and legal paperwork, licensing, and the earliest creative development of the company's brand, including the STT logo and the first concepts for Charlie's Grand Band. By the time the company was ready to open its first location the following year, the identity of the brand was already firmly in place.

The Henderson Blvd Building — Before STT

The building that would eventually become Location #1 had a life of its own long before STT Inc. existed. It was originally built in 1942 as a diner, and operated as one for over two decades before being abandoned in 1966 after its owner was unable to keep the business profitable. The property was subsequently sold to a real estate company, sitting vacant for a few years before STT Inc. acquired it on October 6th, 1970, and converted it into Location #1.

Expansion Timeline

Five locations were part of STT Inc.'s plan from the very start. Locations 2 through 5 are noticeably smaller in scale than the flagship Henderson Blvd location, which allowed them to be built and opened more quickly.

Company Phone Number

As a registered national business, STT Inc. holds a toll-free 010 business number under Ălgrøvík's national phone numbering plan. Rather than a random string of digits, the company's guest services line spells out its own values acronym on a standard telephone keypad:

Number+10-010-326-8279
SpellsF-A-N-T-A-S-Y (3-2-6-8-2-7-9)

The 010 prefix marks it as a nationwide toll-free business line rather than a location-specific number, so the same number reaches STT Inc.'s central guest services regardless of which of the 5 locations a caller is trying to reach.

Before the Founding

Kristján J. Jóhannsson and Johnathan R. Matthews first met as classmates in the same university business course, well before either of them had any plans to start a company together. What began as a shared class eventually grew into a partnership, and in 1969 that partnership became Showbiz Time Theater Inc.

HQ Location & Surrounding Area

The STT Inc. headquarters sits at 11th Jackson Ave, Pine Ridge, Evergreen S.A.D., Ălgrøvík — a semi-rural street. The building was originally constructed by a different company and was purchased by STT Inc. in 1998, several years after the company's 3rd location opened in 1989 — by that point STT Inc. had outgrown running corporate operations out of a location office and needed a dedicated headquarters. The building required renovations before it was fit for use as STT Inc.'s corporate offices.

  • A small pond sits directly behind the HQ building and the road, bordered by a thin tree line — a popular spot for staff to take breaks or eat lunch outside
  • Directly across the road from the HQ is the local Post Office, part of Ălgrøvík's federal Ministry of Mail and Parcels
  • Jackson Ave is lightly trafficked, lined with a mix of small offices, a few older farmhouses, and stretches of undeveloped land
  • The road is a two-lane blacktop with no sidewalks for much of its length, typical of the semi-rural stretches around Pine Ridge
  • Because Evergreen is a Special Administration District, signage along Jackson Ave is bilingual — English first, with Ăllgrøvíàn beneath
  • The nearest town center (shops, diner, gas station) is a short drive away; the immediate area around HQ is quiet and mostly residential/light-commercial
  • Staff parking is a small gravel lot beside the HQ building, with overflow parking along the road shoulder during large meetings

Location #1 — Henderson Blvd

Note: STT Inc.'s 5 locations are spread one per district — Location #1 in Evergreen S.A.D., Location #2 in Østháv, Location #3 in Skjærmørk, Location #4 in Fjørálùnd, and Location #5 in Víkthåvèn — alongside the separate HQ, also in Evergreen S.A.D.

The first Showbiz Time Theater location ever built, and today the largest in the chain. Rather than being built from the ground up, the original building was a converted old diner, acquired by STT Inc. on October 6th, 1970, roughly a year and a half after the company was founded. The building has remained the same size since it first opened — the layout described below reflects the location as it was from day one, with no later expansions.

  • Located at 124th Henderson Blvd, Pine Ridge, Evergreen S.A.D. — one of the busiest streets in Pine Ridge
  • Henderson Blvd is a long boulevard lined with storefronts, apartment buildings, and a handful of small neighborhood parks
  • Heavy foot and vehicle traffic year-round, making it a high-visibility flagship spot for the brand
  • Building has 2 floors: Ground Floor and Basement

The Management Office is a single shared room rather than separate offices — the Store Manager's desk and both Assistant Store Managers' desks sit side by side, each partitioned by a divider for a bit of privacy. The room runs along the back hallway, with a long window giving each desk a clear line of sight down the corridor.

Behind the Management Office is a second, smaller office used by Kristján and Johnathan whenever they're on-site — the only way in is through the Management Office itself. It's a modest room by comparison, furnished mainly with one long desk running along most of one wall, shared between the two of them. It's been in use since the location first opened and remains their office at Location #1 to this day.

This is, in fact, the office the two of them use the most day-to-day — they only make use of their HQ offices on Jackson Ave when something specifically requires their presence there.

The original arcade machines weren't bought new — STT Inc. acquired them secondhand from a local arcade that was upgrading to newer cabinets. The deal was struck under a signed agreement, with STT paying off the discounted machines through small installments over a few years, a practical arrangement for a young company still watching its costs closely in its first years.

Starting in 1980, the prize counter began stocking plush toys modeled after the members of Charlie's Grand Band — a stylized design with oversized heads, large eyes, and small bodies, distinct from the animatronics' own show-accurate look. Prize counter items, plushies included, can be obtained either with tickets or purchased outright with cash.

Much of the location's other furnishings were sourced the same frugal way — tables, storage shelving, and other smaller fixtures came largely from liquidation sales by other companies, or from real estate companies clearing out old buildings and offloading leftover furnishings at a discount.

At the bottom of the basement stairwell is a metal bar gate door, requiring a key that staff are issued to access the utility areas. Past the utility rooms, down a short side hallway, is a second door — solid metal, no window, no label — that only the founders hold a key to. Among employees it's simply known as the "archive room". Staff who work maintenance shifts have learned to avoid lingering near it; several have described an unshakeable "creeped out" feeling when passing by, though no one currently on staff has ever seen it opened.

Later C-Suite Additions (2009)

Four additional chief roles were added in 2009, as the company scaled up around the opening of Location #4: Chief Technology Officer, Chief Marketing Officer, Chief Human Resources Officer, and Chief Legal Officer. The CTO and CLO were each promoted internally, having previously served as Department Head of Information Technology and Department Head of Legal & Compliance, respectively, before moving up into the C-Suite.
